# TradeBot — vision (Peter's own words)

> I want a stock trading bot that doubles as a teacher, so we learn together how to
> trade, what to look for, what the best investors in the world do, etc. Not sure if
> I want short term trading or long term trading, perhaps a mix of both? It should
> teach me the principles and fundamentals of trading and explain its strategies, how
> I could do it myself, etc. To sum it up it's like my bot who is also my genius
> professor/teacher. So I can let the bot do its work while also earning a finance
> certification / knowledge base as I use it.

## What that means for the build
- **Two layers:** a quant ENGINE (data → strategies → backtest → metrics → paper trade)
  and a TEACHER layer that turns the engine's own work into grounded lessons.
- **Teach through truth:** every explanation is tied to a real backtest result + a real
  principle + the investors who championed it — never generic, never confabulated.
- **Certification path:** a structured curriculum (modules → lessons) Peter progresses
  through, building a real finance knowledge base.
- **Stocks first, mix of horizons:** each strategy embodies a school of thought (value,
  growth, momentum/trend, mean-reversion, indexing, risk parity…) so running it = a lesson.
- **Honest discipline (carried from EdgeBot):** model fees, no look-ahead, compare to
  buy & hold, validate out-of-sample before believing an edge, prove before risking money.
  Live order placement stays Peter's hand on the trigger.
